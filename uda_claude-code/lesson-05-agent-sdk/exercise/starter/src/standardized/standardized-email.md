### Risk Assessment

**CRITICAL ISSUES - High Risk Contract:**

1. **Missing Party Information:**
   - Customer entity name not identified (only "Team")
   - No legal entity names or addresses
   - No authorized signatories identified

2. **Undefined Scope:**
   - "Website redesign services" is extremely vague
   - No deliverables list
   - No acceptance criteria for what constitutes "completion"
   - No revision policy or limits

3. **Missing Legal Protections:**
   - No liability limitations or caps
   - No indemnification clauses
   - No warranty disclaimers
   - No dispute resolution mechanism
   - No governing law specification

4. **IP Rights Not Addressed:**
   - No intellectual property ownership terms
   - Unclear who owns final designs, code, assets
   - No license grants specified

5. **Financial Risks:**
   - No late payment penalties or interest terms
   - No refund policy if project is cancelled
   - What happens to deposit if vendor fails to deliver?

6. **Operational Gaps:**
   - No termination rights defined
   - No change order process
   - No timeline for payment processing
   - 6-week timeline has no milestone breakdown
   - No definition of "start" or "completion"

7. **Confidentiality & Data:**
   - No NDA or confidentiality terms
   - No data protection or privacy provisions
   - No security requirements

8. **Format Issues:**
   - Informal email format, not a proper contract
   - No signature mechanism
   - No acceptance procedure defined
   - Phrase "Let me know if you want to proceed" suggests this is still a proposal, not a binding agreement

**RECOMMENDATION:** This informal email should NOT be treated as a binding contract. A formal written agreement should be drafted that addresses all the critical missing terms above before any work commences or deposit is paid.
